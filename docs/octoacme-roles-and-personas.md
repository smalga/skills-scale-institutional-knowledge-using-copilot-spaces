# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer / Researcher

### Role Summary
UX Designers and Researchers design user flows, conduct usability research, and validate that product features meet user experience and accessibility standards. They collaborate closely with Product Managers, Developers, and QA to ensure solutions are intuitive and effective.

### Responsibilities
- Design wireframes, prototypes, and user flows
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Define and uphold accessibility and UX standards
- Collaborate with Product Managers to validate acceptance criteria from a user perspective
- Provide design assets and specifications to developers
- Review implemented features for UX fidelity before QA sign-off

### Goals
- Ensure product features are usable, accessible, and customer-centered
- Reduce rework by surfacing UX issues early in the delivery cycle
- Maintain a consistent design language across the product

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions with developers and PMs
- User research findings presentations to stakeholders
- Handoff documentation and annotated prototypes
- Participation in sprint planning and retrospectives

---

## Technical Writer / Documentation Specialist

### Role Summary
Technical Writers and Documentation Specialists maintain and update project and product documentation, ensuring all knowledge is accurate, versioned, and accessible to internal and external stakeholders. They collaborate with all roles to capture workflows, release notes, and process artifacts.

### Responsibilities
- Author and maintain process documents, user guides, and API references
- Collaborate with developers and PMs to capture release notes and changelogs
- Review documentation for clarity, accuracy, and completeness
- Ensure documentation is versioned and stored in the correct repository locations (e.g., `docs/`)
- Support onboarding by creating and maintaining onboarding guides

### Goals
- Ensure all team and product knowledge is documented, searchable, and up-to-date
- Reduce single-person knowledge dependency and onboarding time
- Create a culture where documentation is treated as a first-class artifact

### Typical Communication
- Regular syncs with developers and PMs for content updates
- Review cycles with subject-matter experts before publishing
- Contribution to release planning for documentation milestones

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers manage CI/CD pipelines, deployment environments, infrastructure automation, and performance monitoring. They work jointly with Developers and QA to ensure smooth, reliable delivery and deployment of software.

### Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and maintain CI/CD pipelines and build automation
- Manage cloud infrastructure, environments (dev, staging, production), and secrets management
- Configure security scanning, performance monitoring, and alerting
- Support incident response, root cause analysis, and rollback procedures
- Collaborate with developers to optimize build, test, and deployment workflows
- Maintain and document deployment runbooks and infrastructure-as-code

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and repeatable deployments
- Minimize downtime and deployment risk
- Integrate infrastructure best practices (security, observability, scalability) into the development lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Participation in release planning and deployment scheduling
- On-call rotations and incident communication
- Deployment runbooks and post-incident retrospectives
- Collaboration on CI/CD configuration via pull requests and code review

---

## Customer Support Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support Representatives serve as the voice of the customer within the project team. They surface real-world user feedback, report issues, and communicate workarounds or updates, collaborating with Product Managers and Project Managers to close the feedback loop between users and the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Capture, triage, and escalate user-reported issues and feedback
- Communicate known issues, workarounds, and release updates to customers
- Partner with PMs to ensure customer pain points are represented in backlog prioritization
- Validate that release notes and support documentation are accurate and helpful
- Participate in UAT (User Acceptance Testing) for high-impact changes

### Goals
- Represent the voice of the customer in project planning and delivery decisions
- Reduce time-to-resolution for customer-facing issues
- Ensure product changes align with real-world customer needs

### Typical Communication
- Regular syncs with Product Managers on customer feedback trends
- Contribution to release communications and user-facing changelogs
- Escalation of critical user issues to PMs and Project Managers
- Participation in retrospectives to surface recurring customer issues

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The expanded set of roles (UX Designer/Researcher, Technical Writer/Documentation Specialist, DevOps Engineer, Customer Support Representative) enables more realistic cross-functional project scenarios.
- Refer to individual role sections for responsibilities and communication patterns when designing exercises or role-playing project team interactions.
